## Support JellyRock

Hiya, I'm Charlie 👋

JellyRock is an open-source Jellyfin client for Roku. I build it on my own, in my spare time: the [app](https://jellyrock.app), its [companion server plugin](https://jellyrock.app/companion-plugin/) and the [docs](https://docs.jellyrock.app). My focus is making it stable and fast, playing your files in their original quality whenever your Roku can, and adding the features people ask for most, without making it harder to use. Sponsorships go straight back into the project: the Roku devices I test on, the domain and email, and the AI coding tools that help me get more done in the time I have. And honestly, it's really encouraging to know people like it that much.
