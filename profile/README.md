JellyRock is a fast, fluid Jellyfin client for Roku, focused on stability, performance, and a polished UX. Free and open-source, no ads, no tracking. Bring your own Jellyfin server.

Running your own Jellyfin server? The optional [JellyRock Companion plugin](https://github.com/jellyrock/jellyfin-plugin-jellyrock) adds casting to a closed app, remote control on HTTPS servers and faster cleanup when you press Home.

## Get help

- **Have a question?** Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/jellyrock/jellyrock/discussions). Other users on [Matrix](https://matrix.to/#/#jellyrock-app:matrix.org) can often help too.
- **Found a bug or have an idea?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/jellyrock/jellyrock/issues/new/choose).
- **Found a security issue?** Report it privately, as the [security policy](https://github.com/jellyrock/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) describes.

## Support JellyRock

I build JellyRock on my own, in my spare time: the app, its companion server plugin and the docs. My focus is making it stable and fast, playing your files in their original quality whenever your Roku can, and adding the features people ask for most, without making it harder to use. Sponsorships go straight back into the project: the Roku devices I test on, the domain and email, and the AI coding tools that help me get more done in the time I have. And honestly, it's really encouraging to know people like it that much.
